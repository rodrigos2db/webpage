Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Version: 1.0

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service (the "Agreement") between Speak2DB ("Processor") and the user ("Controller").

For users who accepted the Terms of Service, this DPA is effective as of the date of acceptance. A signed PDF version is available upon request at info@speak2db.com.

1. Definitions

Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meaning given in the Agreement or, if not defined therein, in applicable data protection laws including but not limited to:

  • Chilean Law 21.719 on the Protection of Personal Data.
  • EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — Regulation 2016/679).
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA).
  • Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Lei 13.709/2018).
  • Chinese Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL).
Controller
The user who determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.
Processor
Speak2DB, which processes personal data on behalf of the Controller.
Personal Data
Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that may be processed under the Agreement.
Sub-processor
A third-party engaged by the Processor to process personal data on behalf of the Controller.

2. Scope and Purpose

The Processor shall process personal data only for the following purposes:

  • Processing database metadata, statistical summaries, and limited data samples to interpret natural-language queries and generate AI responses.
  • Authenticating the Controller's identity and managing their license.
  • Sending essential service communications.
  • Ensuring the security and integrity of the platform.

3. Categories of Personal Data

The Processor may process the following categories of personal data:

Category Data Elements Source
Identity Data Full name, role, company, email, country Provided by Controller during registration
Technical Data IP address, device identifier Collected automatically
Database Metadata Table names, column names, data types, relationships Provided by Controller's database connection
Data Samples Limited fragments of records necessary for pattern identification Provided by Controller's database connection
Statistical Metadata Value summaries, distributions, frequencies Derived from Controller's database

4. Data Subject Categories

The personal data processed may concern the following categories of data subjects:

  • Controller's end users — if data samples contain personal data of third parties, the Controller warrants it has the necessary legal basis to share such data with the Processor.
  • Controller's employees or representatives — email, name, role, device identifier for licensing.

5. Processor's Obligations

The Processor shall:

  • Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required by law to do otherwise (in which case the Processor shall inform the Controller unless legally prohibited).
  • Ensure that persons authorized to process the personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.
  • Not engage another sub-processor without prior notice and the Controller's right to object (see Section 7).
  • Assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests.
  • Notify the Controller of a personal data breach without undue delay (within 72 hours for GDPR-governed data).
  • Delete or return all personal data after termination of the service.

6. Controller's Obligations

The Controller shall:

  • Ensure that the processing of personal data complies with applicable laws.
  • Obtain all necessary consents and legal bases before sharing data samples containing personal data of third parties.
  • Not upload sensitive data (health, biometric, financial, criminal records) unless necessary and with documented legal basis.

7. Sub-processors

The Controller acknowledges and agrees that the Processor engages the following sub-processors to provide the service:

  • DeepSeek AI (China / international) — LLM inference (v4-pro, v4-flash, direct via Speak2DB proxy, ZDR-compliant).
  • Google LLC (USA / EU) — LLM inference (Gemini via Vertex AI, direct via Speak2DB proxy) and cloud infrastructure.
  • OpenRouter, Inc. (USA) — LLM aggregation and routing (ZDR enabled).
  • Zhipu AI / z-ai (China / international) — LLM inference (z-ai/glm-5.2 via OpenRouter).
  • DeepSeek AI (China / international) — LLM inference (deepseek-v4-flash via OpenRouter).

The current list with retention policies and jurisdictions is maintained at speak2db.com/contract/legal/subprocessors. The Processor shall notify the Controller at least 30 days before engaging any new sub-processor, during which the Controller may object.

8. Technical and Organizational Security Measures

The Processor implements at minimum the following measures:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Access controls, authentication, and logging of administrative access.
  • Zero Data Retention (ZDR) compliance across all LLM inference endpoints (direct and OpenRouter-routed).
  • Ephemeral processing — data is discarded immediately after response generation.
  • Annual security reviews and penetration testing of the platform.

9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, the Processor shall:

  • Notify the Controller without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach (for GDPR-governed data; otherwise within reasonable time).
  • Provide the Controller with sufficient information to allow the Controller to meet its notification obligations to data subjects and authorities.
  • Cooperate with the Controller in investigating and remediating the breach.

10. Data Subject Rights

The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection). If a data subject contacts the Processor directly, the Processor shall forward the request to the Controller without delay.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Upon termination of the Agreement, the Processor shall, at the choice of the Controller, delete or return all personal data within 30 days, unless retention is required by applicable law. The Processor shall certify deletion upon request.

12. Audits

The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA once per calendar year, at the Controller's expense, upon 30 days' written notice. Audits shall be conducted in a manner that does not disrupt the Processor's operations. The Processor shall cooperate in good faith.

13. Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of Chile. Disputes shall be resolved in accordance with the arbitration clause set forth in Section 10 of the Terms of Service.

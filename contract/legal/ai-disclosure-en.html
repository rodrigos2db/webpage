AI Disclosure — How Speak2DB Uses Artificial Intelligence

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Version: 3.0

1. This Service Uses Artificial Intelligence

Speak2DB uses large language models (LLMs) to interpret natural language queries and translate them into actionable database insights. The AI system generates responses automatically based on your database schema, statistical metadata, and limited data samples. All providers used by Speak2DB comply with Zero Data Retention (ZDR).

2. What Models Are Used and How They Are Routed

Speak2DB uses two routing paths for AI model queries:

2.1. Direct Route (Speak2DB LLM Proxy, ZDR-compliant)

Queries are sent directly from Speak2DB's backend to:

  • DeepSeek — deepseek-v4-pro, deepseek-v4-flash (direct API)
  • Google Vertex AI — gemini-2.5-flash, gemini-2.5-pro (enterprise API)

These providers are bound by direct data processing agreements with Speak2DB and have contractual zero-retention and no-training terms. All are ZDR-compliant.

2.2. OpenRouter Route (ZDR)

Queries are routed through OpenRouter (an LLM aggregator, USA) to downstream providers selected automatically based on availability, cost, latency, and query complexity. OpenRouter is configured with Zero Data Retention (ZDR), which prevents all downstream providers from retaining or training on your data. Providers include:

  • z-ai — z-ai/glm-5.2
  • DeepSeek — deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash
  • Additional providers may be added based on pricing and availability

2.3. Direct Provider Mode (BYOK)

You may also choose Direct Provider Mode, where you connect using your own API credentials to OpenAI (GPT-5.4) or DeepSeek (v4 Pro). In that mode, Speak2DB does not process your queries (see Section 6).

3. Zero Data Retention (ZDR)

All AI providers used by Speak2DB comply with Zero Data Retention (ZDR). This means your data is never stored, retained, or used for training by any provider.

For the OpenRouter route, ZDR is enforced through OpenRouter's configuration settings, which block any endpoint that retains or trains on request data. For direct providers (DeepSeek API, Google Vertex AI), the same zero-retention and no-training protections are guaranteed through direct contractual DPAs.

Data is processed in real time and discarded immediately after the response is generated. It is not persisted in any database, file system, or log.

4. Limitations of AI-Generated Responses

AI-generated responses may contain errors, omissions, or unintended outputs. By using Speak2DB you acknowledge:

  • Responses are not human-reviewed unless explicitly stated.
  • You are responsible for verifying outputs before acting on them.
  • Speak2DB is not liable for business decisions based solely on AI-generated outputs.
  • The quality of responses depends on your database schema, data quality, and the inherent limitations of the selected model.

5. Decision-Making and Automated Processing

Speak2DB does not make automated decisions with legal or significant effects solely by AI. All queries require user initiation, and all SQL operations require explicit user confirmation before execution. No profiling or automated scoring is performed.

6. Direct Provider Mode (BYOK)

In Direct Provider Mode, you connect using your own API credentials. In this mode:

  • Speak2DB does not process, store, or transmit your queries or data.
  • The third-party provider's terms and privacy policy govern your data.
  • You are the data controller and must ensure compliance with applicable laws.
  • Currently supported direct providers: OpenAI (GPT-5.4) and DeepSeek (v4 Pro).

7. Transparency Per Query

Each AI-generated response in the Speak2DB application displays:

  • A notification that the content is AI-generated.
  • The specific model name that processed the query (e.g., "gemini-2.5-flash", "deepseek-v4-pro", "z-ai/glm-5.2").
  • An indication of the routing path: direct (ZDR) or via OpenRouter (ZDR).

8. Your Rights Regarding AI Processing

  • Right to be informed: You have the right to know which AI system processes your data, what data is used, and which safeguards apply.
  • Right to object: You may object to AI processing by discontinuing use of the service.
  • Right to human review: While outputs are not reviewed by default, you may request a human review of specific results by contacting us.
  • Right to explanation: You may request an explanation of how the AI arrived at a particular output.

9. Compliance Statements

EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) — Article 50

Speak2DB complies with the transparency obligations of the EU AI Act. Users are informed that they are interacting with an AI system. This disclosure is provided before first use and is reiterated with each AI-generated response.

Colorado AI Act (SB 24-205)

Speak2DB provides conspicuous disclosure of AI-generated content and informs users of the models and providers involved in processing their queries.

Chile — Law 21.759 (Artificial Intelligence Regulation)

Speak2DB applies the principle of algorithmic transparency, informing users about the AI system, its purpose, the data processed, and the safeguards in place.

Chile — Law 21.719 (Personal Data Protection)

Speak2DB processes technical data for AI queries under the legal basis of contract performance, with strict privacy-by-design and privacy-by-default measures.

GDPR (Articles 13, 14, 22)

Speak2DB informs users of automated processing, provides meaningful information about the logic involved, and does not make solely automated decisions with legal or significant effects.

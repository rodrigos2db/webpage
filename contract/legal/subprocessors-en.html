1. What Is a Sub-processor?

A sub-processor is a third-party service provider engaged by Speak2DB to process personal data or technical data on our behalf. Speak2DB remains the data controller for your information. Sub-processors are bound by data processing agreements (DPAs) that restrict their use of your data to the purposes specified by Speak2DB.

3. Model Routing Architecture

Speak2DB uses two routing paths for AI model queries:

  • Direct route (ZDR-compliant): Queries are sent directly from Speak2DB's backend to DeepSeek API and Google Vertex AI. All direct providers are ZDR-compliant and bound by data processing agreements with contractual zero-retention and no-training terms.
  • OpenRouter route (ZDR): Queries for z-ai/glm-5.2 and additional deepseek providers are routed through OpenRouter (USA). OpenRouter is configured with Zero Data Retention (ZDR), which prevents all downstream providers from retaining or training on your data. Additional providers may be added through OpenRouter based on availability and pricing; all will be subject to the same ZDR configuration.

The specific model and provider used for a given query may vary depending on availability, cost, latency, and query complexity.

4. International Transfers and Safeguards

Sub-processors may process data in the following jurisdictions:

  • United States — Google Vertex AI, Google Cloud Platform, OpenRouter (aggregator).
  • European Union — Google Vertex AI EU endpoints, under GDPR adequacy.
  • China / International — DeepSeek API (direct) and providers routed through OpenRouter (z-ai/glm-5.2, deepseek via OpenRouter). All are protected by:
    • Contractual safeguards (DPAs with direct providers; OpenRouter DPA for routed providers).
    • Verified Zero Data Retention (ZDR) for all inference endpoints.
    • No-training contractual warranties.

5. Your Rights Regarding Sub-processors

  • Right to be informed: You have the right to know which entities process your data and in which countries.
  • Right to object: You may object to a sub-processor change by contacting info@speak2db.com within 30 days of notification.
  • Right to opt out (where technically feasible): Speak2DB offers an enterprise tier where routing can be restricted to providers with strict DPA terms (Google Vertex AI). Contact us for details.

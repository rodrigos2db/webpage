2. Information We Collect

Speak2DB offers its service through several channels. The categories below describe what we collect per channel. The same LLM infrastructure is used across all channels (see Sub-processor List and AI Disclosure).

2.1. Beta Access Request — Landing Page (speak2db.com/beta.html)

When you request access to the limited beta through our landing page, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Corporate email address
  • Company or organization
  • Role or position (e.g., Inventory Manager, Sales Manager, HR Manager, Operations, IT, Other)
  • Country
  • LinkedIn profile URL (optional)
  • IP address, user-agent and Cloudflare IP (collected automatically for fraud prevention)
  • reCAPTCHA v3 assessment (Google) and Turnstile verification where applicable
  • Registration date and status (pending / approved / rejected)

This data is stored in Firestore (beta_requests/{email} and upon approval beta_testers/{email}) and is administered through an internal back-office. Approval generates a time-limited beta token sent to your email.

2.2. Desktop App — Active Use (Windows beta)

Once approved and using the desktop application, we process:

  • Email address (authentication and account identification)
  • One-time verification token and derived session tokens (JWT / refresh token, hashed at rest)
  • Device identifier — a hash derived from hardware characteristics of your machine (e.g., motherboard serial + CPU ID), used solely for license binding
  • Trial status, license state and token version
  • App version and platform (collected via version-check endpoint for update delivery and aggregated telemetry)
  • Support logs — only if you choose "Send Logs": your email, description and a ZIP of local logs. Connection strings and secrets are redacted before sending; logs are deleted after the support case is resolved
  • Local encrypted storage only — account tokens in an OS-level vault (Stronghold) and connection settings in an AES-encrypted file under your user directory; not transmitted except as above

2.3. Web Demo (demo.speak2db.com)

The web demo is a stateless trial without account creation:

  • Uploaded Excel file (.xlsx) — stored transiently in Google Cloud Storage and automatically deleted ~10 minutes after upload; schema (sheet/column names and types) is held in a short-lived session for query processing
  • Session metadata (schema, language, consent version, IP address) in Firestore with a ~10-minute TTL; IP-based daily query counter (retained up to 30 days for abuse prevention, then deleted)
  • Opt-in email (optional, dismissible) — if you subscribe, your email, language and source ("demo") are stored in demo_subscribers/{email} and added to our Brevo contact list for product updates (only with explicit consent; you may unsubscribe at any time)
  • Cookie consent preference (s2db_cookie_consent) and language preference, stored locally in your browser

2.4. Excel Add-in (Microsoft Office)

The Excel add-in operates in metadata-only mode:

  • Email address and one-time OTP code (6 characters) for authentication; session tokens (JWT / refresh token) stored in Office roaming settings / local storage
  • Consent version and anonymous device identifier (speak2db.anonid.v1) for anonymous rate limiting
  • Database metadata sent to the backend — sheet/column names, data types and relationships; for Insights/consistency features, limited statistical summaries and up to 50 sample rows; the workbook contents never leave your device outside these scoped payloads
  • Monthly usage counters per email (addin_monthly_usage/{email__YYYY-MM}, limit 40 queries/month) and operation IDs for quota enforcement
  • Local telemetry counters (query counts, rate-limit state) and query history stored inside the Excel document properties — no personal data leaves the device

2.5. Technical Data Processed by the AI Service

As described in our Terms of Service and AI Disclosure, Speak2DB uses artificial intelligence models to interpret natural-language queries. When you use the service, we process:

  • Database structure (tables, columns, data types, relationships).
  • Limited data samples to identify content patterns.
  • Statistical metadata (value summaries, distributions).

This technical data is processed solely for the purpose of delivering the service and is not used for profiling or marketing purposes.

2.6. Zero Data Retention (ZDR)

All AI providers used by Speak2DB comply with Zero Data Retention (ZDR). This means your data is never stored, retained, or used for training by any provider.

For queries routed through OpenRouter, ZDR is enforced through configuration that blocks any endpoint that retains or trains on request data. For direct providers (DeepSeek API, Google Vertex AI), the same zero-retention and no-training protections are guaranteed through direct contractual DPAs.

Data is processed in real time and discarded immediately after the response is generated. It is not persisted in any database, file system, or log. For details, see our Sub-processor List.

2.7. Direct Provider Mode (BYOK)

Speak2DB offers an optional Direct Provider Mode that allows you to connect using your own third-party API credentials (currently OpenAI and DeepSeek). In this mode:

  • Speak2DB does not process, store, or transmit your queries or data.
  • The third-party provider's terms and privacy policy govern your data.
  • You are the data controller and are responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable laws (Ley 21.719, GDPR, CCPA, PIPL, etc.).

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Beta request data (landing page): Until approval/rejection and for the duration of your account; if you request deletion, we delete within 30 days unless retention is required by law. The beta token expires per the approval email; download count is tracked with a limited number of uses.
  • Desktop app account data (email, device identifier, trial state): For the duration of your active license and up to 30 days after deletion request.
  • Web demo — uploaded Excel and session: File and session metadata are deleted automatically ~10 minutes after upload. IP-based daily counters are retained up to 30 days for abuse prevention, then deleted by a scheduled job.
  • Web demo — opt-in subscribers: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion (Brevo list + Firestore record).
  • Add-in — monthly quota counters: Per-email counters reset monthly; operation IDs and counters are retained per billing period for quota enforcement.
  • Query processing data (database metadata, samples): Not stored. Data is processed in real-time and discarded immediately after the response is generated. It is not persisted in any database, file system, or log.
  • Device identifier: For the duration of your active license.
  • Statistical records: Only aggregated, anonymous data is retained (query volume, response times, error rates) for service improvement purposes; Cloud metrics up to 90 days.
  • Support logs: Optionally sent by the user, they exclude all database connection parameters, and are deleted once the support case is resolved.

Once data is no longer needed for the purposes described above, it will be securely deleted or irreversibly anonymized, in compliance with the security and proportionality principles of Law 21.719.

8. User Rights

Under Chilean Law 21.719 on the Protection of Personal Data (and, where applicable, the GDPR, CCPA, or LGPD), you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure (Suppression): Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Opposition: Object to the processing of your data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and request its transfer to another controller.
  • Right to Block Processing: Request a temporary suspension of data processing while a rectification, erasure, or opposition request is being resolved.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Chilean Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales) or the corresponding authority in your jurisdiction.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@speak2db.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

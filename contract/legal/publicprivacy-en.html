Speak2DB Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is Speak2DB, based in Santiago, Chile.

For any privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your rights, please contact us at: info@speak2db.com.

2. Information We Collect

Speak2DB collects and processes the following categories of information:

2.1. Registration Data (Trial/Beta Request)

When you register to request access to the software, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Role or position
  • Company or organization
  • Email address
  • Country
  • IP address (collected automatically)
  • Registration date (collected automatically)

2.2. Data During Active Use of the Software

Once approved and using the application, we process:

  • Email address (for authentication and account identification)
  • Device identifier (generated from hardware characteristics of your machine)

2.3. Technical Data Processed by the AI Service

As described in our Terms of Service, Speak2DB also processes:

  • Database structure (tables, columns, data types, relationships).
  • Limited data samples to identify content patterns.
  • Statistical metadata (value summaries, distributions).

This technical data is processed solely for the purpose of delivering the service and is not used for profiling or marketing purposes.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on the following legal grounds, in accordance with Chilean Law 21.719 on the Protection of Personal Data and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

Processing Activity Legal Basis
Registration and account creation Consent of the data subject
Processing database metadata and samples for AI queries Performance of a contract
Sending essential service communications (e.g., license expiration) Performance of a contract
Sending promotional or informational communications Explicit consent of the data subject
Device identification for license management Performance of a contract
Compliance with legal obligations Legal obligation

4. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used for the following purposes:

  • Evaluating and approving access requests to the software.
  • Authenticating your identity and managing your license.
  • Essential communications related to the service (e.g., subscription expiration, security alerts).
  • Sending promotional communications or product updates only with your explicit prior consent. You may withdraw this consent at any time.
  • Ensuring the security and integrity of the platform through device identification.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We share your data with the following categories of third-party service providers, bound by data processing agreements:

Third Party Category Purpose Location
LLM Providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic) Processing natural language queries against database metadata United States
Cloud Infrastructure (Google Cloud) Backend processing and request routing (no user data is stored) United States

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data is shared only as necessary to provide the service or as required by law.

6. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Chile, particularly the United States, where our infrastructure and LLM providers operate.

In accordance with Chilean Law 21.719 (Articles 27 and 28), such transfers are protected by:

  • Contractual clauses that ensure an adequate level of data protection.
  • Data Processing Agreements with all third-party providers.
  • Technical measures including encryption in transit and at rest.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Registration data: For the duration of your account and up to 30 days after deletion request.
  • Query processing data (database metadata, samples): Not stored. Data is processed in real-time and discarded immediately after the response is generated. It is not persisted in any database, file system, or log.
  • Device identifier: For the duration of your active license.
  • Statistical records: Only aggregated, anonymous data is retained (query volume, response times, error rates) for service improvement purposes.
  • Support logs: Optionally sent by the user, they exclude all database connection parameters, and are deleted once the support case is resolved.

Once data is no longer needed for the purposes described above, it will be securely deleted or irreversibly anonymized, in compliance with the security and proportionality principles of Law 21.719.

8. User Rights

Under Chilean Law 21.719 on the Protection of Personal Data (and, where applicable, the GDPR, CCPA, or LGPD), you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure (Suppression): Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Opposition: Object to the processing of your data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and request its transfer to another controller.
  • Right to Block Processing: Request a temporary suspension of data processing while a rectification, erasure, or opposition request is being resolved.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Chilean Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales) or the corresponding authority in your jurisdiction.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@speak2db.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

9. Security Measures

In compliance with the security principle established in Law 21.719, we implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms.
  • Secure storage of credentials (hashed passwords, no plaintext storage).

While we strive to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a security incident affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the Chilean Personal Data Protection Agency without undue delay, as required by Law 21.719.

10. Privacy by Design and by Default

Speak2DB applies the principles of privacy by design and by default as established in Article 14 quater of Law 21.719. We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the service, and our systems are designed to protect personal data from the outset.

11. Children's Privacy

Speak2DB is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

12. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated at least thirty (30) days in advance via email or through a prominent notice within the application.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when this policy was last revised.
